About Me

My first computer was a hand-me-down that barely ran. It froze constantly, and nobody else was going to deal with it, so I did. The first time it stayed up for a whole afternoon was because I found the actual cause instead of guessing, and that felt better than anything else that year.

I went with IT instead of computer science because CS starts with theory, and I had already spent years with my hands in actual machines. Systems work was the obvious fit. Right now I run a single Proxmox host with VMs and Docker containers, and I am rebuilding my home network on UniFi with separate VLANs for trusted devices, IoT, and guests.
